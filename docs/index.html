02 FallinLegacy Anime upscaling, in Soft and Strong variants Scale: 2× Format: ONNX 04 Feb 2026

Notes

Two approaches to anime upscaling. Soft focuses on denoising and compression removal. Strong adds more sharpening.

Soft is the recommended starting point. Strong can introduce oversharpened lines and lighten faces.
